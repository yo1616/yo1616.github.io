Problem & method

Recency, frequency, and monetary value alone may overlook canceled or incomplete transactions. The team explored adding successful-order indicators to RFM and discussed how the resulting segments could inform differentiated marketing.

My contribution

As team lead, I allocated tasks, contributed to some basic analysis, and organized the written report. This does not imply sole ownership of the model, data processing, or all visualizations.

03

Outcomes

The deliverable is a team report on customer evaluation and marketing recommendations. Numerical benefit claims in the report lack sufficient calculation and validation context for reuse here; the page does not claim realized revenue gains or reduced returns.
