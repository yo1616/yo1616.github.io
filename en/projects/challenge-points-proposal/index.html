Project overview

The team began with scoring problems in challenge-based events: inconsistent rules between stations, errors in manual totals, and time-consuming final calculations. The proposal outlined shared scoring rules, live rankings, and announcements for three roles. Administrators would oversee totals, rankings, scoring limits, and announcements; station hosts would enter scores; participants would view their team’s points. Potential settings included school camps and company team activities. This work is presented as a concept proposal, without claims of completed software, user testing, or deployment.

I participated as a team proposal member. This case presents the problem definition and planned features.
