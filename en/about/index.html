ABOUT / PATRICK

About Me

I'm Patrick Tsai (TSAI CHENGYU), currently studying Information Management at National Taiwan University. I'm particularly interested in the intersection of technology and business, and how information systems and data analytics can help address practical problems.

Through coursework and hands-on projects, I've developed an interest not only in technical implementation but also in problem framing, requirements analysis, and system integration. I believe an effective technical solution should do more than function correctly; it should reflect user needs, fit into real workflows, and provide meaningful value.

As the project lead of an AI-powered learning assistant platform, I conducted requirements interviews, planned system architecture and REST API specifications, and coordinated development across different modules. I've also gained experience in Python data processing, statistical modeling, technical analysis, and user research through machine learning, financial data analysis, and HCI projects.

Beyond technical projects, I've participated in budgeting and event planning through student organizations, learning how to coordinate different stakeholders and work with teams under time and resource constraints.

I'm currently exploring career paths in AI products, technology consulting, business analytics, and data analytics. I hope to build on my interdisciplinary Information Management background by developing skills that connect business problems, technical implementation, and practical applications.
